# عفش مصر · Afsh Misr

**Afsh Misr** ("Egypt's furniture") is a single-page site, in Egyptian Arabic, that works out a fair price for wedding furniture in Egypt. For each room it estimates the real cost of materials and labour, the reasonable margin for a workshop and for a showroom, and compares that with IKEA Egypt and ready-made pieces. It also writes the spec and message to send to workshops, compares the quotes you get back, and weighs a nearby workshop against Damietta.

**Live site:** https://iwakiel.github.io/afsh-misr/

Prices are approximate as of October 2026. Material prices change fast, so treat the numbers as a starting point and check them with the supplier or workshop. Your choices are saved only in your own browser.

The whole site is one file, `index.html`, with no build step.
